Selected work

CrewBrief Latest release

Real-time voice AI that turns a crew's pre-job briefing into a signed hazard form.

Utility crews spend around 40 minutes filling in a hazard assessment by hand before every job. CrewBrief listens to the briefing they already hold, drafts the form with evidence for every field, asks about critical hazards nobody covered, and hands it to the crew lead to check and sign. Built on Azure, with safety rules in code rather than prompts.

  • Azure AI Speech
  • Azure OpenAI
  • Azure AI Search

CraftLens 🏆 1st prize

On-device AI coach for hands-on crafts. Won the Enterprise category at the AI On Apple Sydney Hackathon.

Built in 44 hours with a team that met on the Friday night. An iPhone on a tripod watches a trainee barber's hands while the Mac measures the cutting angle 15 times a second and talks them through each step. Apple's on-device Foundation Models answer questions from the salon's own training material, with no cloud, so clients' faces never leave the salon. Any hands-on skill can be next: each new skill is a recipe, not a new app.
